Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 29, 2026 • Platform: Wondrilla AI

1. Overview

At Wondrilla AI (operated by Alrckle), customer satisfaction and trust are paramount. We want you to feel completely confident when subscribing to our premium plans (Pro at $24/month and Studio at $79/month). This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains your rights and our transparent process for cancellations and refund requests.

2. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Peace of Mind Guarantee: If you are new to Wondrilla Pro or Studio and are not completely satisfied with our service, you are eligible for a 100% full refund within 7 calendar days of your initial subscription purchase.

To request a guarantee refund, simply email our support team at support@wondrilla.com or alrckle@gmail.com with your registered account email and order details. No complicated questions asked.

3. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time without fees or penalties:

  • How to Cancel: You can cancel directly from your profile settings in the app, or submit a request to support@wondrilla.com.
  • Continued Access: When you cancel your subscription, auto-renewal is immediately stopped. You will continue to have full access to your Pro or Studio benefits until the end of your current paid billing period.
  • No Lock-In: After your billing period ends, your account will smoothly revert to our Free plan with 20 free monthly messages, and your saved conversation history will remain intact.

4. Duplicate or Erroneous Charges

In the unlikely event of technical error resulting in an accidental duplicate payment, or if an unauthorized charge occurs, please contact us immediately. We will verify the transaction logs and issue a full refund immediately.

5. Refund Processing Timeline

Once approved, refunds are processed immediately on our end:

  • Payment Gateway Timing: The refund is credited back to your original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, Net Banking, or PayPal) via Razorpay or PayPal.
  • Bank Processing Period: Depending on your financial institution or credit card issuer, the funds typically reflect in your statement within 5 to 7 business days.
